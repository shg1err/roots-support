Roots (the "App") keeps what you record inside your device. The developer does not receive your records.

## 1. Information the Developer Collects

**None.**

The App does not collect any information that could identify you, including your name, email address, or device identifiers. It does not perform usage analytics, does not display advertising, and does not share information with third parties.

There is no developer-operated server behind the App. The App itself makes no network requests; the only outbound communication happens through the Apple services described in Section 4.

## 2. Where Your Records Are Stored

Your journal entries, notes, events, reminders, and habit records — along with any images attached to them — are stored **only on your iPhone**.

They are never transmitted off your device. Deleting the App also deletes this data from your device.

## 3. Device Features and Data

The App uses the following only when you turn them on in Settings. All of them are **off by default**.

### Health (Sleep, Body Weight, Step Count)

To show these figures alongside your entries, the App **reads only** from the Health app.

- The figures are **not saved** in the App. They are read again each time a screen is displayed
- They are **never transmitted** off your device
- The App **never writes** to the Health app
- You can stop this at any time from "Settings > Integrations > Health" in the App, or revoke access from "Settings > Privacy & Security > Health" on your iPhone

### Location

The App uses your **while-in-use** location solely to show the weather and your area name on the Today screen.

- Your location is used only to query Apple's weather service (WeatherKit) at that moment and is **not saved** in the App
- No location history is recorded or tracked
- The retrieved weather is also **not saved** (it is held in memory for up to one hour to reduce network use)
- You can stop this at any time from "Settings > Integrations > Weather" in the App

### Face ID and Passcode

If you enable App Lock, the App uses your device's biometric authentication to unlock.

**Your face or fingerprint data is never made available to the App.** iOS performs the evaluation and the App receives only the success or failure result.

### Notifications

All notifications for events, reminders, and daily prompts are **local notifications generated on your device**. Their contents never pass through an external server.

## 4. Apple Services

The App uses the following Apple services. Their handling of data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| WeatherKit | Retrieving weather (see "Location" above) |
| App Store / StoreKit | Purchasing and verifying the Premium plan |

**Purchases are handled by Apple.** The App never receives payment information such as credit card numbers, and the developer has no way to see it. The only thing the App reads is whether the Premium plan is active.

Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/

## 5. Exporting and Deleting Your Data

- **Export**: From "Settings > Data" you can export your records as a JSON file. You choose where to save it. The developer does not receive its contents
- **Delete**: Removing the App from your iPhone deletes the data stored on your device

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at any particular age group. As described above, no information is collected from users of any age.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. If there is a material change to how data is handled, it will be noted in the App's release notes.
