Roots is an app for keeping the record of your daily life in one place — journal entries, notes, events, reminders, and habits — light enough that you'll want to open it every day.

## Contact

**roots.app@outlook.jp**

Please get in touch with bug reports, feature requests, or any other questions.

For bug reports, the following details help a great deal in tracking down the cause:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- Your Roots version (shown under Settings > About)
- Which screen you were on and what you were doing when it happened

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is my data stored?

**Only on your iPhone.** Nothing is sent to a developer server. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for details.

### Can I move my data to a new device?

Yes. Use **Settings > Data** to export and import.

1. On your current device, tap "Export" and save the JSON file wherever you like (iCloud Drive, the Files app, and so on)
2. Install Roots on the new device, tap "Import", and choose that file

Export and import are available on the free plan.

### What happens if I lose my device?

Roots stores data only on your device, so **losing the device means losing the records**. We recommend backing up regularly with "Settings > Data > Export".

### Weather or health figures aren't showing

First, check that the item is turned on under "Settings > Integrations" in Roots. **They are off by default.**

If it's on but still not showing, iOS may not have granted permission:

- Weather: iPhone "Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Roots"
- Health: iPhone "Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Roots"

Note that Integrations are a Premium plan feature.

### Notifications aren't arriving

Check that notifications are allowed under iPhone "Settings > Notifications > Roots". Also confirm that your events and reminders have a date and time set, and that "Settings > Daily reminder" is on in Roots.

### I can't unlock App Lock

If Face ID fails, the App falls back to your device passcode. If that doesn't work either, check that your iPhone passcode is correct. Roots never has a password of its own.

## About the Premium Plan

### What the free plan includes

All of the recording features are free. There are no limits on journal entries, notes, events, reminders, image attachments, export and import, or App Lock. You can create up to 3 habits.

The free plan is limited as follows:

- Theme colors: 4
- Habits: up to 3
- Integrations (Health and Weather): not available
- Monthly and yearly entries: not available
- Widgets: the 4 Home Screen widgets (Today's page, Up next, Habits, That day)

### What Premium adds

- **All 11 theme colors**
- **Unlimited habits**
- **Integrations (Health and Weather)**
- **Monthly and yearly entries** (a place to look back and set goals at the end of each month and year)
- **All widgets** (Memo, Calendar, and Photo widgets, plus the Lock Screen widgets)

Pricing is shown under "Settings > Premium Plan". A **7-day free trial** is included; cancel within the trial period and you won't be charged.

### If you already have more than 3 habits

Even over the limit, **all of your existing habits remain fully visible**, and you can keep recording and editing them. Only adding new ones stops. Archiving a habit you no longer use frees up a slot again.

### How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are cancelled through iOS Settings:

1. Open "Settings" on your iPhone
2. Tap your name at the top
3. Tap "Subscriptions"
4. Select "Roots" and tap "Cancel Subscription"

You can also reach the same screen from "Settings > Premium Plan" inside Roots.

**Cancelling does not delete your records.** Theme colors, habit count, Integrations, and widgets return to the free plan's range, and you can no longer start new monthly or yearly entries, but everything you have recorded — including those entries — stays.

### I purchased Premium but it isn't active

Open the paywall from "Settings > Premium Plan" and tap "Restore Purchases". You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to purchase.
